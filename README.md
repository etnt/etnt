Here follows some if the things I've built, that I'm using myself, almost every day.

### Skyoverhead

[Skyoverhead](https://github.com/etnt/skyoverhead) is an Android app that reads
your current location, then using public OpenSky Network and ADSBDB data to
identify the aircraft flying over your head.

To make it more fun, you can turn on: Collector Mode ,which will log all traffic
you have identified, making various statistics available together with a medal
ranking system for collected destinations.

### My-Football

[My-Football](https://github.com/etnt/my-football) is an Android app that helps
you to follow the major European football leagues. It shows live league
standings, fixtures (recent results and upcoming matches grouped by matchweek),
and per-team schedules.

It requires a Premium Key, purchased from `thesportsdb.com`, and adds Live
scores tab, Player stats leaderboards with top scorers, assists & cards, etc.

### guten-speak

[Guten-Speak](https://github.com/etnt/guten-speak) is an Android app that turns
Project Gutenberg into a personal audiobook library.
Search for public-domain books, download them, read them in a clean e-reader,
and — optionally — have them narrated aloud in a voice you cloned yourself,
entirely on-device.

### Whistle Tab

[Whistle Tab](https://github.com/etnt/whistle-tab) ia s web-based application
that converts ABC musical notation into standard sheet music with either tin
whistle ("Irish flute") or fiddle (violin) tablature display.

Play your tunes with flute or violin sound (based on tab type) and control the
playback tempo from 50% to 150%. Export to MIDI. Run it locally or straight from:
https://whistletab.kruskakli.se/

### SR Podcast

[SR-Podcast](https://github.com/etnt/sr-podcasts) is a small Android app with
a personal short list of Sveriges Radio podcasts. You open the app and tap
a show. The latest episodes appear without a search.

The app appears in Android Auto on the car screen. The car shows its own media
screens, not the app's phone UI. You browse your saved shows, pick an episode,
and control playback with the car's buttons.

### TODO

[TODO](https://github.com/etnt/todo) is both a full-screen todo app for the
terminal and an Android app. Both apps use GitHub issues to store todo notes.

You can use the apps with several GitHub repositories. A private repository
also works for personal lists, such as a shopping list for example.

### Voice Lab

[Voice Lab](https://github.com/etnt/voice-lab) provides local voice cloning
and speech generation (TTS).

It comes with two built-in reference voices — pick a voice, then type sentences
one at a time and get a `.mp3` file. Provide your own voice as a `.wav` sample.
To be used, e.g to narrate your video creations.
